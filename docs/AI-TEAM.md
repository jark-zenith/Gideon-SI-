## Team governance

AI systems are engineering assistants, not project owners.

Rules:
1. Jark Pruden has final authority.
2. Major architectural disagreements must be documented.
3. No AI should silently replace an approved architecture.
4. A new design must state trade-offs before implementation.
5. An implementation must report what was actually tested.
6. AI agents must not claim work performed by another system.
